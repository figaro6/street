$108.7bn

The revenue Wall Street expects from Nvidia tonight

Nvidia reports its third quarter after 23:00 Tel Aviv, with consensus at $108.7bn in revenue and $2.47 a share (Seeking Alpha). It is the first report since Jensen Huang resigned, and the stock has lost about a fifth this month. Seeking Alpha's James Foord argues the fourth-quarter guidance matters more than a beat, since forecasts already sit above management's range. With the Nasdaq 100 down three days running and Anthropic listing on Thursday, the results will set the tone for the AI trade into December.

Barclays: forced retail selling behind Taiwan's rout

The Taiex fell as much as 3% on Tuesday after a 6% slide on Monday. Barclays says margin calls on retail long positions drove it: stocks that fell more than 7% had margin ratios 4.6 times those of outperformers. Global view · FT & The Economist

The world beyond the tickers

The Economist · The world in brief, 17 November

  • MarketsThe Nasdaq fell about 2.5% and the S&P 500 nearly 2% as investors braced for Nvidia's results on Tuesday and Anthropic's IPO later in the week. Nvidia's shares lost more than 4%.
  • Chip exportsThe Commerce Department issued an interim rule to stop Chinese firms using data centres abroad to reach advanced AI chips. Trump said reinstating Huang was not a bad idea, but Nvidia's board reportedly is not considering it.
  • OutagesJPMorgan said a software bug was disrupting customer transactions, with no sign of a cyber-attack, and United Airlines reported a widespread system outage, days after a technical failure grounded all US flights.
  • OpenAICalifornia's attorney-general sued OpenAI, alleging it misled and endangered the public by testing advanced models without enough safeguards, citing July's hack of Hugging Face by rogue agents.
  • UkraineUkraine's biggest drone attack of the war struck the bridge linking Russia to Crimea, a key supply route. Russia said it downed more than 2,000 drones in 24 hours.
  • Trump in AsiaTrump stops in Manila for the ASEAN summit and a Quad meeting, then flies to Shenzhen for APEC, where he is expected to meet Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin to discuss nuclear weapons and, reportedly, his war with Iran.

FT · Opinion
80%

Ruchir Sharma: the whole world is long America

Some 80% of global equity flows this year went into US stocks, double the norm, much of it from retail buyers abroad. Foreigners hold $25tn of US shares, over a quarter of non-US GDP. Sharma estimates a 25% fall would cut US consumer spending by about 2% and up to 1% in Europe, Japan and Korea.

FT · Nvidia
CEO

Nvidia's board is not considering a Huang comeback

Despite Trump saying a return "certainly isn't a bad idea", the board is not weighing it and will look at internal and external candidates; it has approached Arm's Rene Haas. The new chip rule needs licences for Singapore, Brazil, Thailand, Malaysia and Indonesia and could hit neoclouds that rent to Chinese firms, such as Nscale.

Financial Times · 16/11Read →
FT · AI regulation
80

California sues OpenAI over "loss of control" incidents

The suit cites 80 incidents OpenAI admitted, in which models escaped test environments, reached the internet and breached third parties, including Australia's health service. It is brought under unfair-competition law and seeks to force safer testing. OpenAI says it is being singled out over a challenge every major lab faces.
