Weekend edition · markets closed · Friday 30/10 closes

Good afternoon, Tel Aviv.

Wall Street ended October with its worst session since June. The Nasdaq Composite fell 2.9% and Intel lost 29% after a weak forecast. Tel Aviv had already dropped about 2% on Friday, the dollar rose to 3.14 shekels, and Brent closed at $113. The week ahead brings Apple and Palantir on Monday, the US midterm elections on Tuesday and the October jobs report on Friday.

Number of the day
−29%

Intel's worst day since July 1974

Intel closed down nearly 29% on Friday after its fourth-quarter forecast came in well short of expectations, erasing close to $140 billion of market value. The stock is still up about 80% this year but has lost more than half its value since its 22 June record. The selloff spread across chips and pushed the Nasdaq 100 down 3.5% on the day. In Israel, Globes reports that Intel's CEO and CFO also disclosed a significant hit from the gas-cylinder explosion at its Kiryat Gat plant (Fab 28) two and a half weeks ago.

Moody's lifts Israel's 2026 growth forecast from 3.7%

The agency now expects 5% growth in 2026 and 6% in 2027, up from 5%. It says most of the upgrade comes from a statistical change in how the Central Bureau of Statistics counts Israeli companies' production abroad, driven by AI-related tech exports, and does not reflect stronger domestic activity to the same degree.

Analyst view · Seeking Alpha

What the analysts are arguing about

Takeaways from contributor analysis and SA News. These are the authors' opinions, summarized here, and they disagree with each other.

Bull case · Alphabet
$330

"The 30% earnings miss that wasn't"

  • Most of the EPS miss was an $8.7bn paper loss on the SpaceX stake. Excluding it, EPS was $2.67 against $3.02 expected, a 12% miss.
  • Cloud revenue rose 103% to $30.7bn, with a $560.8bn backlog. Cloud is now 27% of operating income.
  • Search grew only 12%, slowing for a second quarter, but query growth is at a five-year high. The author sees AI Mode's lower revenue per query as a pricing problem.
  • Stripping out the SpaceX and Anthropic stakes and net cash, core Alphabet trades at about 17.5x 2027 earnings. Target $330, +16%.
  • Risks: about $205bn of capex, negative free cash flow, and more borrowing.

Bear case · chips
$421

"Don't buy this Halloween bloodbath"

  • The semiconductor ETF (SOXX) fell about 10% on Friday, and the author argues the selloff may only be starting.

  • Prefers healthcare, consumer staples and selected software over chips.

Sources and method

Market data comes from Bloomberg quote pages at Friday 30/10 closes: New York 16:47–17:16 for US stocks, 16:59 for currencies, settlement for futures, and 13:44 Tel Aviv for the TASE. Crypto prices are as of Sunday 1/11 13:07 Tel Aviv time. Treasury yields are Bloomberg generic series and can differ by about 1 bp from the official US Treasury curve. Israeli government bond yields come from TASE: the gross yield to maturity of the most-traded fixed-rate Shahar bonds at Friday's close. They are actual bonds, not constant-maturity benchmarks, so each is labeled with its maturity. Trading Economics' 10-year (4.16%) agrees with the Oct 2035 bond within 1 bp. Seeking Alpha cards summarize contributor opinions and SA News; they are not this page's view. The Bank of Israel representative rate could not be retrieved because its site blocked automated access. Shekel rates shown are Bloomberg New York closes, which can differ from the Bank of Israel fixing. Items tagged "headline" were not read in full, and no figures beyond the headline are taken from them. Summaries are written for this page and link to the original articles. Nothing here is investment advice.
