News of the day

The stories behind the numbers

US · the close
−0.4%

Stocks and bonds both fall on hike fears

The S&P 500 ended 0.4% lower and the Nasdaq 100 0.6% lower after October's hotter-than-expected CPI, while the dollar rose 0.5%. The 10-year yield climbed to 4.99%; German and UK 10-year yields rose too. Power-equipment stocks such as GE Vernova and Bloom Energy fell after SoftBank wrote down $3.7bn of data-centre projects.

Bloomberg · 10/11Read →
US · Fed
2+

Fed officials line up behind more hikes

After Waller raised the idea of a half-point move, Dallas Fed president Lorie Logan backed at least two more quarter-point hikes, possibly at a faster pace than this autumn. Pimco's Tiffany Wilding sees hikes in December and January, with a risk of another in March. Principal's Seema Shah says a bigger move needs proof that inflation is accelerating. Futures put a December quarter-point hike at about 87%, per Bizportal.

Bloomberg · Bizportal · 10/11Read →
US · earnings
+7%

CoreWeave beats and raises; shares jump late

Revenue of $3.66bn beat forecasts, the loss was smaller than expected and the 2026 revenue guide rose to $13-13.3bn, with adjusted operating profit guidance up 26% at the midpoint. Before the report, Bloomberg noted the stock was 65% below its peak and its bonds yield over 15%. Investors cared most about the outlook after the midterms, where voters pushed back against data centres.

Seeking Alpha · Bizportal · Bloomberg · 10/11Read →
US · Nvidia
−5.5%

Nvidia's board widens its probe into Huang

Nvidia's independent directors hired a former top federal prosecutor from Wachtell to help investigate Jensen Huang's relationship with a suspected Chinese spy. The shares fell to about $168.89. The FT reports the commerce department has delayed adding Megaspeed, the Singapore firm at the centre of the affair, to its trade blacklist. Bloomberg also reports China's Moonshot rented top Nvidia chips this summer (headline).

Anthropic's run-rate revenue hits $81bn

New filings show Anthropic's annualised revenue run-rate reached $81bn in October, up $10bn in a month, as the roadshow began. TSMC's board approved buying up to $500mn of shares in the IPO. Bloomberg reports some backers are offering parts of their allocation to new investors at a big premium, and Anthropic launched new office tools that compete with Microsoft and Google.

Israel · rates
1.6%

US inflation dims hopes for an Israeli rate cut

Israeli inflation is 1.6%, near the bottom of the Bank of Israel's target, and September's CPI fell 0.5%. That would normally argue for a cut at the last decision of the year. But Mizrahi Tefahot's chief strategist says another US hike would weigh heavily against a cut, now or in January. Higher US yields pull money into dollars; the shekel has already weakened 3.1% since the last decision.

Israel · TASE Ltd
+74%

The exchange itself cashes in on busy trading

The Tel Aviv Stock Exchange's nine-month net profit rose 74% to NIS 224.7mn on revenue up 31% to about NIS 544mn, while costs rose only 4%. Market value of listed shares reached NIS 3.1tn, average daily equity turnover rose to NIS 5.2bn, and foreign investors now account for about 40% of trading.

FT · Anthropic
$600bn

Goldman's model: Anthropic sales 130 times higher by 2030

Anthropic's lead bank has told a potential investor it expects revenue of about $52bn this year, $136bn in 2027 and over $600bn by 2030, up from $4.6bn in 2025. But it sees free cash outflows of $65.7bn in 2027 and operating losses peaking at $40.4bn that year, with positive free cash flow only in 2030. Anthropic has committed about $900bn to computing capacity.

Financial Times · 10/11Read →
FT · Alphaville
$32bn

SpaceX's IPO: a small index splash, a big giveaway

Goldman finds SpaceX has barely changed the Nasdaq 100's valuation, volatility or concentration. But index funds, mainly QQQ, have had to buy about $32bn of its shares, with $10bn more due at the next Russell 1000 Growth rebalance. Alphaville argues this hands passive money's cash to early holders. About half the shares become tradeable by summer 2027, when S&P 500 entry becomes possible.

Financial Times · 10/11Read →
FT · Nvidia
3.6%

Can Jensen Huang survive the spy scandal?

Huang owns only 3.6% of Nvidia and has no special voting rights, so the board could remove him. The company has no named chair and its filings describe no CEO succession plan. Director Stephen Neal is expected to lead the probe. Bernstein says Huang "IS Nvidia", while investors warn the stakes rise sharply if any information was compromised.
