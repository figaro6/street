Good afternoon, Tel Aviv.

Jensen Huang has resigned as Nvidia's chief executive, with finance chief Colette Kress taking over for now. At the open Nvidia fell as much as 13%, the Nasdaq 100 lost 1.4% and a chip index 3.5%, but by 17:00 buyers had cut the Nasdaq 100's loss to about 0.5%. Bonds sold off as well: the 2-year Treasury yield is up 10 bp to 4.84%, gold is down 3% and bitcoin 4%, after slightly higher jobless claims and Trump's comment that the US no longer needs a deal with Iran. In Tel Aviv the TA-35 lost a 1% gain when the news broke and is up about 0.3% heading into the close.

Key numbers

Nvidia without Huang: a sharp drop, then a partial rebound

Number of the day
−13%

Nvidia's drop at the open after Huang resigns

Nvidia's board announced that Jensen Huang, who co-founded the company 33 years ago, has resigned after the revelation of two affairs, one of them with a woman US officials suspect of spying for China. CFO Colette Kress is interim chief, and long-time director Harvey Jones becomes Nvidia's first board chair. The shares fell as much as 13% at the open, erasing more than half a trillion dollars, then pared the loss to about 8% ($159). The FT says about $1.5tn of market value has gone since the scandal broke two weeks ago.

News of the day

The stories behind the numbers

US · Nvidia
−13%

Huang resigns; Kress takes over as interim CEO

Nvidia's board said Jensen Huang has resigned and that it will look at internal and external candidates; its statement gives no reason. Harvey Jones, a director since 1993 and an early Huang mentor, becomes the first board chair. The FT lists possible successors including Ian Buck, Arm's Rene Haas and AMD's Lisa Su. Trump said the FBI is "looking into" Huang (FT headline).

FT · Bloomberg · 12/11Read →
US · the open
−3.5%

Chip stocks slide, then buyers step in

Bloomberg says the leadership crisis curbed appetite for riskier assets: at 16:30 the S&P 500 was down 0.8%, the Nasdaq 100 1.4% and a chip index 3.5%, while the Dow edged up. Within half an hour losses had roughly halved. Gabelli's Ryuta Makino warns of a "ripple effect" through the AI supply chain if Nvidia loses market share.

Bloomberg · 12/11Read →
US · analysts
$200

Wall Street cuts Nvidia targets in half

Bernstein cut its target to $200 even before the resignation, citing Nvidia's many investments, backstops and guarantees, but called the shares "almost absurdly cheap". Bespoke counts at least six downgrades on Thursday, some halving targets. One long-time hedge fund holder told the FT the key questions are how many affairs there were, where the chips went, and who succeeds Huang.

FT · Seeking Alpha · 12/11Read →
US · 15:30 data
200k

Jobless claims tick up, but the labour market holds

Initial claims rose to 200,000 from an upwardly revised 191,000, above the 194,000 expected. Continuing claims fell by 20,000 to 1.697mn, below the 1.71mn forecast, and the four-week average was unchanged at 195,250. Bizportal's read: employers are not hiring much, but they are not laying people off widely either.

The 2-year Treasury yield is up about 10 bp to 4.84% and the 10-year is at 5.05%, while gold fell 3% to about $3,868. Bloomberg's wrap pairs the bond selloff with oil volatility after Trump's Iran comments; traders already fully price a December Fed hike. The shekel weakened 0.6% to 3.194 per dollar.

Nebius dropped about 16%: revenue rose 570%, but a bigger spending plan pushes some 2026 revenue into next year (Seeking Alpha, Bizportal). New-home sales collapse in Tel Aviv

New-home sales in Tel Aviv fell 60% in Q3 from Q2, lifting the city's unsold stock 5%, according to the Central Bureau of Statistics. About 21,800 homes were sold nationwide, down 6%, and Haifa topped the deal count for the first time. Mortgages, though, are near a record year at NIS 97.5bn so far (Bizportal).

Financial Times · key reads

FT · AI financing
$105bn

Huang's exit threatens the web he financed

Under Huang, Nvidia became the AI industry's backer of last resort: an anchor order for CoreWeave's IPO, up to $105bn of guarantees for an OpenAI data centre campus, $36bn of capacity backstops and $124bn of equity commitments. Huang did much of this dealmaking himself. One neocloud executive expects it to largely stop until the uproar settles.

Financial Times · 12/11Read →
FT · Nvidia live
$13bn

From successors to the Swiss central bank

The FT's live coverage notes Nvidia was the only S&P 100 company without a chair until today. The Swiss National Bank held about $13bn of Nvidia stock in June, its largest US holding. Nvidia also plans to invest $10bn in Anthropic alongside next week's IPO, which deepens concerns about circular financing.

Financial Times · 12/11Read →
FT · Alphaville
~$2tn

Bond investors may cap the AI build-out

Goldman now sees $8.5tn of AI capex by 2031, about 35% of it debt-funded, and S&P expects the big hyperscalers' adjusted debt to reach about $2tn by 2029. The total is manageable for the credit market, but strict limits on single issuers mean so much debt from a few names may meet resistance.

Financial Times · 12/11Read →
FT · Israel defence
$70mn+

Eric Trump-backed robot maker courts the IDF

Foundation, a humanoid-robot start-up where Eric Trump is chief strategic adviser and a shareholder, is in talks with Israel's military. It and a company it bought have received more than $70mn from the US military. Democrats, who take control of Congress in January, have criticised its Pentagon contract; the company denies any Trump influence.
