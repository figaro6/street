0.9%

The S&P 500 move options price for the day after the midterms

Friday's tech rout pushed the VIX to its highest intraday level since April, lifted the cost of puts and raised correlations between stocks. But Bloomberg reports that the options market's price for the election itself has barely budged for weeks, at roughly a 0.9% move for the session after Tuesday. Traders see AI earnings (Nvidia later in November) and Iran talks as the bigger swing factors. Some are buying calls on beaten-down tech and call spreads on airlines and GE, a bet on a Middle East ceasefire. One caveat from an OptionMetrics study: in years when control of Congress shifts sharply, post-midterm returns have been less reliable.

The stories behind the numbers

US · volatility
Apr

After the tech rout, traders brace for more wild days

Friday's VIX touched its highest intraday level since April as the S&P 500 had its worst day since June, though stress gauges are still far below March, when the Iran war began. Lombard Odier reads it as a rotation out of tech rather than a broad breakdown, and argues calls are a cheap way to keep upside into year-end because hedge funds hold little net exposure. Leveraged tech ETFs could amplify chip moves either way.

US · bonds
$6bn

Dealers brace for the biggest Treasury issuance shift in years

Many dealers expect Wednesday's quarterly refunding statement to drop guidance that has stayed steady for more than two years, opening the door to smaller sales of the longest bonds in 2027 and bigger bill and short-note auctions. Citi goes further and expects cuts to 20- and 30-year sales to start this month. Most expect buybacks to stay at $6 billion per sector at the long end. Bessent's recent willingness to push yields down is behind the shift.

3

SocGen: three events test the credit-market recovery

Société Générale's Juan Esteban Valencia sees Tuesday's midterms, Friday's jobs report and more earnings as risks to a tentative recovery in corporate bonds. Credit can keep gaining if government bond yields settle and geopolitics stays calm, but a change in control of Congress would raise questions about fiscal policy.

Kiryat Gat explosion proves costly for Intel

A gas cylinder exploded during unloading at Fab 28 two and a half weeks ago, killing two workers. Intel's management has now said the fab, which makes its 10-nanometer chips, was closed for two weeks and was expected to restart this week. Together with a weak PC outlook and cut Q4 guidance, it helped send Intel down nearly 30% over Thursday and Friday.
