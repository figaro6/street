Friday 6 Nov 2026 · 23:40 Tel Aviv
Night edition · Wall Street close 23:00 · TASE close · after-hours earnings

Good night, Tel Aviv.

Wall Street ended an extraordinary week with a lopsided rebound: the Nasdaq 100 gained 1.35% and chipmakers bounced, but the S&P 500 rose only 0.3% and the Dow fell 0.5%, as most S&P members still declined. A strong jobs report (+165,000) kept a December Fed hike in play at about 70%, and Fed's Daly said more hikes are likely. Anthropic is pressing ahead with its IPO at a lower valuation, while Trump named Stephen Miller his chief of staff after the midterm defeat. In Tel Aviv the TA-35 jumped 3.1% and the shekel firmed to 3.19 per dollar.

Edition focus

The close, the corporate news, and Monday's setup

Wall Street's close

  • S&P 500 +0.3%, Nasdaq 100 +1.35%, Dow −0.5%; breadth stayed weak.

Dip buyers lift tech; most stocks still fall

The Nasdaq 100 rose 1.35% and chip giants gained about 4% at the close, but most S&P 500 members declined and the Dow lost 0.5%. Global view · FT & The Economist

The world beyond the tickers

The Economist · The world in brief, 6 November

  • White HouseDays after the Republican midterm defeat, Trump named Stephen Miller, the architect of his immigration policy, as chief of staff. He replaces Susie Wiles, seen as a stabilising force, who moves to the "Trump Political Operation".
  • US economyAmerica added 165,000 jobs in October, up from 60,000 in September and far above forecasts, led by professional services. That makes a Fed rate rise next month more likely. Stocks rebounded after Thursday's sharpest fall in over 18 months.
  • TeslaMusk said Tesla will wind down Cybertruck production at its Texas plant, the only site making it, prompting speculation the model may end entirely. Its lead engineer said it will "live on" through the Cybercab robotaxi.
  • Japan / RussiaJapan protested after Russia seized the local assets of Japan Tobacco International, partly state-owned. Tensions have risen in recent weeks, including the arrest of a Japanese journalist on espionage charges.
  • Law firmsThe lawyer picked to run Weil, Gotshal & Manges will join rival Kirkland & Ellis, hastening the collapse of the century-old firm, which said on 28 October it would close.

FT · Anthropic
$81bn

Anthropic will accept a lower IPO price

Investors say the AI lab plans to list before US Thanksgiving, raising $80–100bn at $1.25–1.75tn versus its current $965bn valuation. Q3 revenue growth slowed but still rose almost 50% to about $17bn, and annualised revenue is now around $81bn. It holds about $150bn of cash and wants to raise enough that it never sells equity again. OpenAI is cutting the valuation and size of its own round.

Financial Times · 6/11Read →
FT · US-China trade
Jan 10

US tells China to meet its Busan trade commitments

Treasury's Bessent and trade envoy Greer told China's He Lifeng on a Friday call to implement the Busan truce "fully and immediately", citing rare-earth exports and farm purchases. The truce deadline was extended to January 2027 in September. The warning comes about two weeks before an expected Trump-Xi meeting at the Shenzhen APEC summit. Japan's yttrium stockpiles are reportedly running low.

Abu Dhabi

Europe alarmed by a US-Russia security "framework"

After meeting Russia's envoy Dmitriev, Trump said a big agreement would involve "more than Ukraine", and Kushner spoke of a long-term European security framework. European capitals object to negotiating the continent's architecture inside Ukraine talks. US, Russian and Ukrainian negotiators meet in Abu Dhabi next week, though sources doubt a meaningful ceasefire. EU leaders discuss a united approach at a summit.

Cautious · market
3-sigma

The path forward after this mini-meltdown

  • The 4% S&P drop is a rare 3-sigma event, following a momentum collapse since early July.
  • Weakness is broad; value, staples and homebuilders held up better.
  • Drivers: political uncertainty and fears of an AI buildout slowdown.
  • Advice: keep a 10–20% cash cushion and hedge selectively unless a recession arrives.
